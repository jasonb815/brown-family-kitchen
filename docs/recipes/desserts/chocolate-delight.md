# Chocolate Delight

<p class="recipe-intro">A layered chilled dessert with a buttery nut crust, a sweet cream cheese layer, chocolate pudding, and a Cool Whip topping.</p>

<div class="recipe-meta" markdown>

| Bake | Difficulty |
|---|---|
| 15-20 minutes | Easy |

</div>

!!! tip "Cool the crust"
    Let the baked crust cool completely before adding the cream cheese layer so it doesn't melt.

## Ingredients

### Crust

- **1 1/2 cups** self-rising flour
- **1 stick** margarine, melted
- **1 cup** chopped nuts

### Cream Cheese Layer

- **1 (8 oz)** package cream cheese
- **1 cup** confectioners sugar
- **1/2** of a 16 oz container Cool Whip

### Pudding and Topping

- **1 large box** instant chocolate pudding
- **3 cups** milk
- **The remaining 1/2** of the Cool Whip, for the top

## Directions

1. Preheat the oven to **350°F**.
2. Mix the flour and melted margarine together and spread the mixture into a casserole dish. Press the chopped nuts into it.
3. Bake for **15 to 20 minutes** at 350°F, then let the crust cool completely.
4. Mix the confectioners sugar, cream cheese, and half of the Cool Whip until smooth. Spread it over the cooled crust.
5. Whisk the milk and instant chocolate pudding together until thick, and spread the pudding over the cream cheese layer.
6. Chill until set, then top with the rest of the Cool Whip.

## Brown Family Notes

- Added to the cookbook to try.
- From Christy Shaw, in a community cookbook.
- The original doesn't give a dish size, servings, or chill time. A casserole dish that holds the crust in a thin even layer works, and chilling for a few hours or overnight should set it.
- The source lists "(16-oz.) Cool Whip" without a count. This recipe assumes one 16 oz container: half goes in the cream cheese layer and half on top.
