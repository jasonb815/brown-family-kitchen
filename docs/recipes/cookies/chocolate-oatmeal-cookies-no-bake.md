# Chocolate Oatmeal Cookies (No-Bake)

<p class="recipe-intro">Old-school no-bake chocolate peanut butter oatmeal cookies.</p>

<div class="recipe-meta" markdown>

| Oven | Bake | Makes |
|---|---|---|
| No oven | — | About 24 cookies |

</div>

## Ingredients

- **1/2 cup** butter
- **2 cups** sugar
- **1/2 cup** milk
- **1/4 cup** cocoa
- **3 cups** oats
- **1 cup** peanut butter
- **1 tsp** vanilla

## Directions

1. Boil the butter, sugar, milk and cocoa together for **1 minute**.
2. Stir in the oats, peanut butter and vanilla.
3. Drop spoonfuls onto a surface and let them cool until set.
