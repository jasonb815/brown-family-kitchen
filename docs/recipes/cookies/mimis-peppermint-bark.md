# Mimi's Peppermint Bark

<p class="recipe-intro">A holiday white and dark chocolate peppermint bark that disappears fast.</p>

<div class="recipe-meta" markdown>

| Oven | Bake | Makes |
|---|---|---|
| No oven | — | About 1 pan |

</div>

## Ingredients

- White chocolate
- Dark chocolate
- Peppermint chips
- Peppermint oil

## Directions

1. Melt the chocolates.
2. Layer them and add the peppermint flavor.
3. Chill, then break into pieces.
