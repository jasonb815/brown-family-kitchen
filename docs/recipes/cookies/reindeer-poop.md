# Reindeer Poop

<p class="recipe-intro">Ridiculous but good: chocolate peanut butter cereal clusters rolled in powdered sugar.</p>

<div class="recipe-meta" markdown>

| Oven | Bake | Makes |
|---|---|---|
| No oven | — | About 20 clusters |

</div>

## Ingredients

- **1 cup** chocolate chips
- **1/2 cup** peanut butter
- **1 1/2 cups** Chex cereal
- **1 cup** powdered sugar

## Directions

1. Melt the chocolate chips and peanut butter together.
2. Stir in the Chex cereal.
3. Coat the clusters in powdered sugar.
