# Paleo Chocolate Crinkle Cookies

<p class="recipe-intro">Rich chocolate cookies made with almond flour, coconut oil and maple syrup.</p>

<div class="recipe-meta" markdown>

| Oven | Bake | Makes |
|---|---|---|
| 350°F | 10-12 minutes | About 12 cookies |

</div>

## Ingredients

- **1/4 cup** coconut oil
- **1/4 cup** maple syrup
- **2 tsp** vanilla
- **1 cup** almond flour
- **1/4 cup** cocoa
- **1/2 tsp** baking soda
- **1/4 tsp** salt

## Directions

1. Preheat the oven to **350°F**.
2. Mix the coconut oil, maple syrup and vanilla.
3. Add the almond flour, cocoa, baking soda and salt.
4. Scoop the dough and bake for **10 to 12 minutes**.
