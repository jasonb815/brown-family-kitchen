# Sugar Cookies (Cut-Out)

<p class="recipe-intro">A classic holiday roll-and-cut sugar cookie.</p>

<div class="recipe-meta" markdown>

| Oven | Bake | Makes |
|---|---|---|
| 350°F | 8-10 minutes | 30-40 cookies |

</div>

## Ingredients

- **1 cup** butter
- **1 cup** sugar
- **1** egg
- **1 tsp** vanilla
- **3 cups** flour
- **1 tsp** baking powder
- **1/2 tsp** salt

## Directions

1. Preheat the oven to **350°F**.
2. Cream the butter and sugar.
3. Add the egg and vanilla.
4. Mix in the dry ingredients.
5. Roll out the dough, cut into shapes, and bake for **8 to 10 minutes**.
