# Christmas Crack Cookies

<p class="recipe-intro">Brown-butter cookies loaded with toffee bits and chocolate chunks.</p>

<div class="recipe-meta" markdown>

| Oven | Bake | Makes |
|---|---|---|
| 350°F | 8-10 minutes | About 16 cookies |

</div>

## Ingredients

- **1/2 cup** butter
- **1/2 cup** brown sugar
- **1/4 cup** sugar
- **2 tbsp** yogurt
- **1** egg
- **1 tsp** vanilla
- **1 1/4 cups** flour
- **1/2 tsp** baking soda
- **1/2 tsp** salt
- **1/2 cup** toffee bits (Heath Bar bits may be used instead)
- **1/2 cup** chocolate chunks

## Directions

1. Preheat the oven to **350°F**.
2. Brown the butter.
3. Mix in the sugars.
4. Add the yogurt, egg and vanilla.
5. Add the flour, baking soda and salt.
6. Fold in the toffee bits and chocolate chunks.
7. Bake for **8 to 10 minutes**.
