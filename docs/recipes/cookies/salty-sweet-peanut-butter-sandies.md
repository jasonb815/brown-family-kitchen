# Salty-Sweet Peanut Butter Sandies

<p class="recipe-intro">Peanut butter cookies finished with a sprinkle of flaky salt and sugar.</p>

<div class="recipe-meta" markdown>

| Oven | Bake | Makes |
|---|---|---|
| 350°F | 12-15 minutes | 3-4 dozen cookies |

</div>

## Ingredients

- **1 cup** butter
- **3/4 cup** sugar
- **3/4 cup** brown sugar
- **1 tsp** kosher salt
- **2 cups** peanut butter
- **2** eggs
- **2 cups** flour
- Flaky salt + sugar, for sprinkling

## Directions

1. Preheat the oven to **350°F**.
2. Cream the butter and both sugars.
3. Add the peanut butter and eggs.
4. Add the flour and kosher salt.
5. Scoop the dough onto a baking sheet.
6. Sprinkle with the flaky salt and sugar.
7. Bake for **12 to 15 minutes**.
