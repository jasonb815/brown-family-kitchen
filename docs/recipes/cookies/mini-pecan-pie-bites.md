# Mini Pecan Pie Bites

<p class="recipe-intro">Bite-size pecan pie treats with Thanksgiving energy.</p>

<div class="recipe-meta" markdown>

| Oven | Bake | Makes |
|---|---|---|
| 350°F | 15-18 minutes | About 24 bites |

</div>

## Ingredients

- Brown sugar
- Butter
- Eggs
- Vanilla
- Pecans
- Flour

## Directions

1. Preheat the oven to **350°F**.
2. Mix the ingredients together.
3. Bake for **15 to 18 minutes**.
