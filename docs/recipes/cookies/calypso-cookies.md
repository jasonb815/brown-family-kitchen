# Calypso Cookies

<p class="recipe-intro">Oatmeal cookies loaded with pecans, chocolate chips and coconut.</p>

<div class="recipe-meta" markdown>

| Oven | Bake | Makes |
|---|---|---|
| 350°F | 10-12 minutes | About 24 cookies |

</div>

## Ingredients

- **1/2 cup** butter
- **1/2 cup** granulated sugar
- **1/2 cup** brown sugar
- **1** egg
- **1 tsp** vanilla
- **1 1/4 cups** flour
- **1/2 tsp** baking soda
- **1/4 tsp** salt
- **1 cup** oats
- **1/2 cup** pecans
- **1/2 cup** chocolate chips
- **1/2 cup** coconut

## Directions

1. Preheat the oven to **350°F**.
2. Cream the butter and both sugars.
3. Add the egg and vanilla.
4. Add the dry ingredients.
5. Fold in the oats, pecans, chocolate chips and coconut.
6. Bake for **10 to 12 minutes**.
