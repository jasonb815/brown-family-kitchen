# 100 Grands

<p class="recipe-intro">No-bake chaos bars with rice cereal, peanuts, coconut, marshmallows, chocolate and butterscotch.</p>

<div class="recipe-meta" markdown>

| Oven | Bake | Makes |
|---|---|---|
| No oven | — | About 20 bars |

</div>

## Ingredients

- Rice cereal
- Peanuts
- Coconut
- Marshmallows
- Chocolate chips
- Butterscotch chips

## Directions

1. Melt the chocolate and butterscotch chips.
2. Stir the melted chips into the dry ingredients.
3. Chill until set.
