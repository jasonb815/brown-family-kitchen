# 3-Ingredient Shortbread Cookies

<p class="recipe-intro">Simple, buttery shortbread with just butter, powdered sugar and flour.</p>

<div class="recipe-meta" markdown>

| Oven | Bake | Makes |
|---|---|---|
| 325°F | 10-12 minutes | About 24 cookies |

</div>

## Ingredients

- **1 cup** butter
- **1/2 cup** powdered sugar
- **2 cups** flour

## Directions

1. Preheat the oven to **325°F**.
2. Cream the butter and powdered sugar.
3. Mix in the flour.
4. Chill the dough for **40 minutes**.
5. Bake for **10 to 12 minutes**.
