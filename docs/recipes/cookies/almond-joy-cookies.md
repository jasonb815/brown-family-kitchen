# Almond Joy Cookies

<p class="recipe-intro">Chewy cookies with coconut, chocolate chips and a whole almond on top, with all the candy bar nostalgia.</p>

<div class="recipe-meta" markdown>

| Oven | Bake | Makes |
|---|---|---|
| 350°F | 12-14 minutes | About 24 cookies |

</div>

## Ingredients

- **1 cup** unsalted butter
- **1 cup** granulated sugar
- **1 cup** brown sugar
- **2** eggs
- **1 1/2 tsp** vanilla
- **2 1/2 cups** flour
- **1 tsp** baking soda
- **1/2 tsp** salt
- **1 cup** coconut
- **1 cup** chocolate chips
- **1 cup** whole almonds

## Directions

1. Preheat the oven to **350°F**.
2. Cream the butter and both sugars.
3. Add the eggs and vanilla.
4. Mix in the dry ingredients.
5. Fold in the coconut and chocolate chips.
6. Press a whole almond on top of each cookie.
7. Bake for **12 to 14 minutes**.
