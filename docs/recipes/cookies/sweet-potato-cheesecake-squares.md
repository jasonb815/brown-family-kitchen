# Sweet Potato Cheesecake Squares

<p class="recipe-intro">Gingersnap-crust cheesecake squares with a sweet potato filling.</p>

<div class="recipe-meta" markdown>

| Oven | Bake | Makes |
|---|---|---|
| 350°F | 40-45 minutes | About 16 squares |

</div>

## Ingredients

- Gingersnap crust
- Cream cheese
- Sweet potatoes
- Eggs
- Spices

## Directions

1. Preheat the oven to **350°F** and bake the crust.
2. Add the filling.
3. Bake for **40 to 45 minutes**.
