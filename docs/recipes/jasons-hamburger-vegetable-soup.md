# Jason's Hamburger Vegetable Soup

*A hearty pantry soup that's become a Brown family favorite. Brown the beef, open a handful of cans, add water, and let it simmer.*

## Ingredients
- 2 lbs ground beef
- 1 (15 oz) can Ranch Style Beans, undrained
- 2 (14.5 oz) cans Italian-style diced tomatoes, undrained
- 1 (14.5 oz) can cut green beans, undrained
- 1 (15.25 oz) can whole kernel corn, drained
- 1 packet Hidden Valley Ranch Seasoning Mix
- 2 cups water

## Directions
1. Brown the ground beef and drain the grease.
2. Add all remaining ingredients.
3. Bring to a boil.
4. Reduce heat and simmer for 30-45 minutes.
5. Serve hot.

## Brown Family Notes
- Dad always doubles the hamburger.
- One of our favorite pantry meals.
- Even better the next day.
- Great with cornbread, saltines, or grilled cheese.

## Optional Hearty Potato Version
- Add 3-4 diced Yukon Gold potatoes.
- Leave the corn undrained.
- Simmer until potatoes are tender.
