# Frozen Banana Yogurt Pops

<p class="recipe-intro">A simple bite-sized frozen snack made with banana and yogurt, plus whatever toppings sound good. The small size makes these especially handy for toddlers and younger kids.</p>

<div class="recipe-meta" markdown>

| Prep | Freeze | Difficulty |
|---|---|---|
| 10 minutes | Overnight | Easy |

</div>

!!! tip "Toddler-sized"
    Keep the banana slices about **1/2 inch thick** so they stay sturdy on the sticks. The smaller size also makes them easier for little kids to finish than a whole frozen banana pop.

## Ingredients

- **1 banana**
- **Yogurt**, enough for dipping

### Optional Toppings

- Freeze-dried strawberries, crushed
- Crushed peanuts
- Coconut flakes
- Chocolate drizzle
- Peanut butter, as an alternative to yogurt for dipping

## Directions

1. Slice the banana into pieces about **1/2 inch thick**. Don't slice them too thin.
2. Insert a lollipop stick into each banana slice without pushing it all the way through.
3. Dip each banana slice into yogurt or peanut butter.
4. Sprinkle or drizzle with your favorite toppings.
5. Place the pops on a parchment-lined tray or plate and freeze **overnight**.
6. Serve frozen.

## Brown Family Notes

- Added to the cookbook to try as an easy kid snack.
- Make several at once and keep them in the freezer for grab-and-go snacks.
- Two small pops make a convenient toddler-sized portion; adjust based on appetite.
- For younger children, supervise closely when serving anything on a lollipop stick.
- If using peanuts or peanut butter, make sure they are appropriate for the child and avoid large or coarse nut pieces that could be a choking hazard.
