# Alton Brown's Brined Turkey

<p class="recipe-intro">A whole turkey soaked overnight in a spiced vegetable-stock brine, then roasted hot and finished low for juicy meat and crisp skin. The brine is made a few days ahead.</p>

<div class="recipe-meta" markdown>

| Prep | Brine | Roast | Serves |
|---|---|---|---|
| 50 minutes | 8-16 hours | 2-2 1/2 hours | 12 |

</div>

!!! tip "Plan ahead"
    Thaw the turkey in the refrigerator 2 to 3 days ahead and make the brine stock the same day. The turkey goes into the brine the night before you roast it.

## Ingredients

### Turkey

- **1** whole turkey, 14 to 16 lb, thawed

### Brine

- **1 cup** kosher salt
- **1/2 cup** light brown sugar
- **1 gallon** vegetable stock
- **1 tbsp** black peppercorns
- **1 1/2 tsp** allspice berries
- **1 1/2 tsp** chopped candied ginger
- **1 gallon** water, heavily iced

### Aromatics and Roasting

- **1** red apple, sliced
- **1/2** onion, sliced
- **1** cinnamon stick
- **1 cup** water
- **4 sprigs** fresh rosemary
- **6** fresh sage leaves
- Canola oil, for coating the skin

## Directions

### 2 to 3 Days Before

1. Thaw the turkey in the refrigerator, or in a cooler kept at 38°F.
2. In a large stockpot over medium-high heat, combine the vegetable stock, salt, brown sugar, peppercorns, allspice and candied ginger. Stir until dissolved and bring to a boil.
3. Take the pot off the heat, let the brine cool to room temperature, then refrigerate it.

### The Night Before

1. In a clean 5-gallon bucket, combine the chilled brine, the water and plenty of ice.
2. Remove the giblets and neck from the turkey. Place the turkey breast side down in the brine, weighing it down if needed so it stays fully submerged.
3. Cover and keep refrigerated or very cold for **8 to 16 hours**, turning the bird once about halfway through.

### Roasting Day

1. Preheat the oven to **500°F** and set a rack on the lowest position.
2. Remove the turkey from the brine, rinse it inside and out with cold water, and discard the brine.
3. Set the turkey on a rack in a roasting pan and pat it completely dry with paper towels.
4. Microwave the apple, onion, cinnamon stick and 1 cup water in a microwave-safe dish on high for **5 minutes**. Put the steeped aromatics in the cavity along with the rosemary and sage.
5. Tuck the wings under the bird and coat the skin generously with canola oil.
6. Roast at 500°F for **30 minutes**.
7. Insert a probe thermometer into the thickest part of the breast and tent the breast with a foil triangle. Lower the oven to **350°F**.
8. Keep roasting until the thermometer reaches the target temperature (see notes). A 14 to 16 lb bird takes about 2 to 2 1/2 hours of total roasting time.
9. Rest the turkey for **15 minutes**, loosely covered with foil or a large bowl, before carving.

## Brown Family Notes

- Added to the cookbook to try.
- Brining helps keep the breast meat tender and moist.
- The original sets the thermometer alarm at 161°F, relying on the temperature rising while the turkey rests. The USDA safe temperature for poultry is 165°F, so confirm the thickest part of the breast has reached 165°F after resting before serving.
- Use a food-safe bucket or brining bag, and keep the turkey cold the whole time it is brining.
- Discard the brine after use. Never reuse it.
