# Jason's Hamburger Vegetable Soup

<p class="recipe-intro">A hearty Brown family pantry soup. Brown the beef, open the cans, add water, and let it simmer.</p>

<div class="recipe-meta" markdown>

| Prep | Cook | Serves | Difficulty |
|---|---|---|---|
| 10 minutes | 30-45 minutes | 8-10 | Easy |

</div>

!!! tip "Why we love it"
    Other than browning the beef, there is almost no real cooking involved. Everything else comes straight from the pantry.

## Ingredients

- **2 lbs** ground beef
- **1 (15 oz) can** Ranch Style Beans, **undrained**
- **2 (14.5 oz) cans** Italian-style diced tomatoes, **undrained**
- **1 (14.5 oz) can** cut green beans, **undrained**
- **1 (15.25 oz) can** whole kernel corn, **drained**
- **1 packet** Hidden Valley Ranch Seasoning Mix
- **2 cups** water

## Directions

1. Brown the ground beef in a large Dutch oven or stockpot.
2. Drain the excess grease.
3. Add the Ranch Style Beans, tomatoes, green beans with their liquid, drained corn, ranch seasoning, and water.
4. Stir well and bring to a boil.
5. Reduce the heat and simmer for **30-45 minutes**, stirring occasionally.
6. Serve hot.

## Brown Family Notes

- Dad always doubles the hamburger from the original recipe.
- The **corn is drained**, but the **green beans are not**.
- It is even better the next day.
- It freezes well.
- Serve with cornbread, saltine crackers, or grilled cheese sandwiches.

## Optional Hearty Potato Version

Add **3-4 medium Yukon Gold potatoes**, cut into small cubes.

For the potato version:

- Leave the corn **undrained** to provide additional liquid.
- Add the potatoes with the canned ingredients and water.
- Simmer until the potatoes are fork-tender, about **30-35 minutes**.
